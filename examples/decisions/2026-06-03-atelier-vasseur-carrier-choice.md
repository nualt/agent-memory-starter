# Atelier Vasseur chose Colissimo over Chronopost for shipments in France

## What happened

Atelier Vasseur, 2026-06-03: the workshop needed a single carrier for
shipments in France before the summer season. Two offers were on the
table, Colissimo and Chronopost. Monthly volume at the time was under
two hundred parcels.

## Why

Atelier Vasseur chose Colissimo because its per-parcel price at that
volume was lower and its pickup schedule matched the workshop's opening
days. Chronopost was rejected because its rate only became competitive
above five hundred parcels a month, a volume the workshop did not
expect before 2027.

## Rule

Atelier Vasseur reviews the carrier choice every quarter against actual
monthly volume, and switches to Chronopost if volume passes five hundred
parcels for two consecutive months.

## Where it now lives

The Atelier Vasseur carrier rule is not yet promoted to a procedure.
