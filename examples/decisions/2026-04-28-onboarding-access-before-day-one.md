# New hire accounts are created before day one, not on day one

## What happened

Sales team, 2026-04-28: a new sales representative spent the first
morning without email, without the CRM and without a badge. The manager
opened three tickets at 9 am, IT handled them by 2 pm, and the new hire
spent the afternoon catching up on what the morning should have covered.

## Why

New hire accounts were created on the start date because nothing
triggered IT earlier. The contract was signed weeks before, so the
information existed; it simply reached IT too late. Creating accounts
before day one costs IT nothing extra and removes the one visible
failure a new hire remembers.

## Rule

New hire accounts are requested five working days before the start date
and confirmed two days before.
