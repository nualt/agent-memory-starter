# New hire onboarding

New hire onboarding solves the first-week problem for any new employee:
arriving without accounts, without a contact, and without knowing where
the procedures are. It was first applied on the sales team on
2026-05-12.

## When to apply new hire onboarding

New hire onboarding starts from a signed contract with a start date,
for any role, employee or contractor.

## Principles of new hire onboarding

1. Accounts exist before day one. Why: a person who waits for access on
   their first morning learns that nothing is ready for them.
2. One named contact for the first month. Why: questions go somewhere
   instead of nowhere.
3. The procedures the role uses are listed, not described. Why: the new
   hire reads them at their own pace.

## Steps of new hire onboarding

New hire onboarding runs in four steps.

1. Five working days before the start date, the manager sends the access
   list to IT. Output: a ticket with role, start date, and systems.
2. Two days before, IT confirms accounts and hands credentials to the
   manager. Output: a confirmation on the ticket.
3. On day one, the manager introduces the named contact and shares the
   procedure list for the role. Output: the new hire has the list and
   the contact's name in writing.
4. At the end of week one, the contact checks that every system on the
   list has been opened at least once. Output: a short note on the
   ticket.

## Checks for new hire onboarding

Every account on the access list works on day one. The new hire can
name their contact. The procedure list matches the role.
