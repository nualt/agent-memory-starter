# Atelier Vasseur

Atelier Vasseur is a leather goods workshop that sells online and ships
its own parcels from a single site.

## Atelier Vasseur: stack and hosting

Atelier Vasseur sells through a hosted shop platform and manages
shipments from the platform's back office. Orders are exported weekly to
the accounting tool.

## Atelier Vasseur: people

Atelier Vasseur is run by its founder, who handles orders and shipping.
Escalation for the shop goes to the studio that built it.

## Atelier Vasseur: decisions

- 2026-06-03: Atelier Vasseur uses Colissimo for shipments in France
  because its price at low volume was lower; Chronopost was rejected
  because its rate only wins above five hundred parcels a month. See
  `decisions/2026-06-03-atelier-vasseur-carrier-choice.md`.

## Atelier Vasseur: known constraints

- Atelier Vasseur ships on workshop opening days only, Tuesday to
  Saturday, because pickups depend on someone being on site.

## Atelier Vasseur: open items

- 2026-06-03: Atelier Vasseur reviews the carrier choice each quarter.

## Atelier Vasseur: history

- 2026-06-03: Atelier Vasseur chose Colissimo for France.
